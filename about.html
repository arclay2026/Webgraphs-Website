About

Practical solutions, creative results.

Web Graphs Technologies was built to solve a simple problem: people and businesses often need to run between different providers for services that should be easy to find in one place — from getting fingerprinted for a driving platform, to printing a document, to building a brand from the ground up.

Our story

Based in Langlaagte North, Johannesburg.

We combine technical services with creative design and training, giving individuals and businesses a reliable partner for both the practical and the creative sides of getting things done.

Our mission

Making tech & design accessible to everyone.

  • AccessibilityQuality services and skills training, without the runaround.
  • TrustHonest pricing and clear communication on every job.
  • GrowthHelping clients and students grow their businesses and skills.
