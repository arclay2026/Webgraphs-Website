About Web Graphs Technologies

A Johannesburg-based company bridging the gap between everyday tech needs and creative excellence.

Our Story

Practical Solutions, Creative Results

Web Graphs Technologies was built to solve a simple problem: people and businesses often need to run between different providers for services that should be easy to find in one place — from getting fingerprinted for a driving platform, to printing a document, to building a brand from the ground up.

Based in Langlaagte North, Johannesburg, we combine technical services with creative design and training, giving individuals and businesses a reliable partner for both the practical and the creative sides of getting things done.

Our Mission

Making tech & design accessible to everyone

  • Accessibility Quality services and skills training, without the runaround.
  • Trust Honest pricing and clear communication on every job.
  • Growth Helping clients and students grow their businesses and skills.
